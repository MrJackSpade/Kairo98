Kairo98 Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 25, 2026

Kairo98 is an Android PC-98 emulator published by Loxifi LLC. This policy covers Kairo98, including the paid Google Play edition and the free GitHub edition. For privacy questions or requests, email admin@loxifi.com.

Data on your device
Kairo98 reads the game folder and any firmware files that you choose through Android's file picker. It uses disk image contents to identify supported games. Game files, file names, content hashes, imported firmware, settings, and downloaded images stay on your device in the selected folder or the app's private storage. Loxifi does not receive these files, names, hashes, or settings. Kairo98 has no account, advertising, analytics, or crash reporting service.

Optional artwork downloads
Artwork is not bundled in the no-artwork edition. If you choose "Download missing images" or tap an image to request a larger version, Kairo98 makes an HTTPS request directly to images.launchbox-app.com or gamesdb-images.launchbox.gg. The requested image URL can reveal which game's artwork was requested. The image service also receives your network IP address and ordinary request information needed to deliver the image. Kairo98 does not send your game files, file names, firmware, or content hashes to these services, and Loxifi does not receive their request logs. The services control their own logging and retention. Downloaded thumbnails are stored on your device.

Storage, security, and deletion
Kairo98 accesses files only after you select them with Android's file picker. It stores app data in private app storage, does not enable Android app backup, and uses HTTPS for optional artwork requests. Local app data remains until you clear Kairo98's storage or uninstall it; that does not remove original game files in your chosen folder. Loxifi has no server copy of app data to delete. To ask about data sent to an image service, contact that service. If you email Loxifi for support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send and use it to respond; email admin@loxifi.com to request deletion of that correspondence.

Changes
We may update this policy when Kairo98's behavior changes. The current version will be available in the app and through the Google Play listing.
