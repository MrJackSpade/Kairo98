# Early game artwork candidates

This records the first two artwork candidates considered for Kairo98. Both were later included as downscaled images in the `withImages` development bundle. A public image URL or the ability to download an image into LaunchBox does not establish permission to redistribute it in Kairo98. Their creator, rights holder, and permission basis for free and paid distribution remain part of the release audit.
